Tips for better answers

Be specific
"Murder rate in Houston, 2020 to 2024" gets a better answer than "crime in Texas."
Name a period
Say "2019 to 2024" instead of "recently."
Ask follow-ups
The assistant keeps the last question. Try "now show that as a rate per 100,000" or "same for Dallas."
Check the question it answered
Every answer shows the question it was read as. Edit it if that is not what you meant.

Before you use the numbers

  • An agency missing from the data may not report; it does not mean zero crime. Months an agency did not report are shown blank, never zero.
  • NIBRS participation varied widely by state and year before 2021. An increase may reflect more agencies reporting, not more crime.
  • The FBI changed the definition of rape in 2013. Compare rape figures across that change with care.
  • Current Trends data is preliminary, collected directly from police departments, and subject to revision.
  • Hate crime reporting is voluntary. Zero does not mean zero hate crimes.
  • Populations are for the whole jurisdiction only, so rates by race, sex or age cannot be calculated.
